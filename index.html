Zero-Overhead Context Modeling via Causal Mamba for High-Fidelity Frequency-Domain Neural Audio Coding

Seorim Hwang1, Seungmin Shin1, Jongmo Sung2, Seungkwon Beack2, Youngcheol Park1

1Yonsei University, Wonju, Korea    2ETRI, Daejeon, Korea

Paper (PDF) GitHub Skip to samples

We propose a strictly autoregressive, zero-overhead hybrid context model for MDCT-based neural audio coding. Masked 2D convolutions and causal ConvNeXt blocks extract local time-frequency textures, and a unidirectional Mamba stack models long-range dependencies along the frequency axis with linear complexity. No hyperprior side information is transmitted. Below, the proposed codec is compared against FDK-AAC (LC) and our previous time-domain NAC at 32, 40, 48 and 56 kbps.
